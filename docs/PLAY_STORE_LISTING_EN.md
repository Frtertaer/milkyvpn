## Full description (≤4000)
MilkyVPN is a minimalist VPN client for Milky subscription holders.

No server lists, ports or protocols. Add your subscription once — then press a single button.

• Auto mode — the app picks a working server and switches automatically on failure.
• Finland and USA — choose a region with one tap.
• Honest status — "Connected" appears only after a real connectivity check through the tunnel.
• Persistent notification with a Disconnect action — you always know when the VPN is on.
• Android Always-on VPN support.
• Light and dark themes.

Privacy:
• No analytics, ads or trackers.
• No traffic or browsing logs.
• Subscription data stays on the device, encrypted with Android Keystore and excluded from backups.
• Support diagnostics are generated on demand and contain no secrets.

A Milky subscription is required. The app does not sell subscriptions and has no in-app purchases. Get a subscription or help: https://t.me/MilkyVPNbot

The app uses Android VpnService and asks for VPN permission through the system dialog before the first connection.
